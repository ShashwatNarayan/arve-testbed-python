- **SEC-01** — JWT signing key bound to a local, then passed to jwt.encode on the next line (line_span 2). Gitleaks reports the assignment and Semgrep the call, via constant propagation. Semgrep does not match a key="..." keyword argument against the rule's positional "..." pattern, so the same-line form was tested and dropped.
- **SEC-02** — AWS key pair on two adjacent lines (line_span 2). Gitleaks reports both lines: aws-access-token on the key ID and generic-api-key on the 40-char secret. Semgrep reports the secret line.
